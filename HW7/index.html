HW 7 Ray Tracing: Christmas Tree and Snow Globe by the Fireplace

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
Everything is ray traced: the fireplace, the Christmas tree (textured tree, bark trunk, ornaments), three wrapped gifts and a red gift tag, and a glass snow globe with a snowman.
Three lights (fire, room, star) with ray-traced shadows; the globe refracts, the floor and ornaments reflect.

Ray tracing concepts in this scene:

    Each pixel shoots a ray into the scene. The ray is tested against spheres, planes, triangles, boxes, cylinders, and cones to find what it hits. When it hits a shiny surface it bounces, and when it hits the glass it bends (refracts) or reflects, using Snell’s law and Fresnel to choose which. Shadow rays check whether a light is blocked, and a tiny offset keeps each new ray from hitting the surface it just left. A loop handles the bounces, since GLSL can’t recurse.
