Christmas Tree: Hierarchical Model with Two Lights

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
Drag with the mouse to rotate the scene. Arrow keys and w, s move the camera.
The star is a second light source that sways with the tree.
Tree cones are flat shaded; the trunk is smooth shaded.
Trunk and presents are diffuse only; the tree is specular (shiny).
